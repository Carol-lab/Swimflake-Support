Swimflake turns the swims your Apple Watch records into a sketchbook. Every swim becomes a ring; milestones arrive as postcards. There is nothing to log.

What it does

Every swim becomes a ring

Its size is the distance, its colour the stroke you swam most, its depth how hard you worked, and the rings inside it are your sets. The wobble comes from the day, so no two swims are drawn alike.

A calendar that fills itself in

Your week, your month or your whole year on one page. A rest day is a quiet dot, never a red mark. Tap a ring for the swim: every set, splits every 100 m, pace, heart rate and effort.

Milestones arrive by post

Thirty postcards to collect: creatures for distance, shells for the weeks you keep going, plants for new bests, and more for when and where you swim. Each one is sent by the swim that earned it, with a note on the back.

Send a swim to a friend

Make a card from any swim, sized for stories, posts or anywhere else. Heart rate, calories and your pool stay off unless you turn them on.

Private by design

Swimflake reads your swims from Apple Health on your iPhone and never writes anything back. No account, no server, no tracking. Privacy policy.

Questions

How do I start?

Swim with your Apple Watch using the Workout app's Pool Swim or Open Water Swim. When the swim reaches Apple Health, it appears in Swimflake. The first time you open the app it asks to read your workouts from Health; it only reads.

Swimflake is empty, but I have swims.

Swimflake probably can't read them. Open the Health app, tap your picture at the top right, then Apps, then Swimflake, and turn on every category. Then open Swimflake again.

One swim is missing.

Swimflake shows swimming workouts. A swim saved as another kind of workout, such as "Other", won't appear. Swims recorded by another app appear too, as long as that app saves them to Health as swimming workouts.

Can I try it without an Apple Watch?

Yes. In the You tab, under Settings, set "reading" to "sample data". Swimflake fills itself with fourteen months of made-up swims so you can see every screen. Set it back to "apple health" to see your own.

Does it do yards?

Not yet. Distances are shown in metres.

How do postcards work?

There are thirty. Each one has a rule, and the swim on which the rule first comes true sends it: the distance you've covered, the weeks you've kept going, new bests, when and where you swim. The "still to write" list shows what's left and how close you are.

Why does saving a card ask about Photos?

To save a share card, Swimflake needs permission to add to your photo library. It can only add; it can't see the photos you already have.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Swimflake keeps is on your iPhone: your name, your monthly goal and a few preferences. Delete the app and it's gone. Your swims themselves stay in Apple Health, where your Apple Watch put them.
