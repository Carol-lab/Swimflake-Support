Privacy policy

effective 14 October 2026

Swimflake does not collect any data. Your swims are read on your iPhone, drawn on your iPhone, and stay there.

The short version

There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Swimflake makes no network connections at all. Nobody, including the developer, can see your swims or anything else you do in the app.

What Swimflake reads

With your permission, Swimflake reads your swimming workouts from Apple Health: when each swim started and ended, its distance, whether it was in a pool or open water, its laps and sets, stroke counts and stroke types, heart rate, active energy and effort rating.

It uses this only to draw your swims and work out your totals, records and postcards, on your iPhone. Health data is never sent anywhere, never stored in iCloud by Swimflake, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone.

Swimflake asks for read access only. It never writes, changes or deletes anything in Apple Health. You can turn its access off at any time in the Health app: tap your picture, then Apps, then Swimflake.

What stays on your phone

Swimflake keeps a few things in its own storage on your iPhone: the name you type in the You tab (if you type one), your monthly goal, which day your week starts, your share-card choices, which postcards you've already seen, and whether you're looking at sample data. None of it leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos

If you save a share card, Swimflake asks for permission to add images to your photo library. That permission lets it add the card and nothing else; it cannot see, read or change the photos you already have.

Sharing a swim

A share card is made on your iPhone and goes only where you send it, through the iOS share sheet. You choose what's on it. Heart rate, calories and your pool are left off unless you turn them on. Once you send a card, the app or person you sent it to has it, under their own terms.

Children

Swimflake collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If Swimflake ever starts to handle data differently, this page will change first, with a new date at the top, and the App Store listing's privacy details will change with it.
